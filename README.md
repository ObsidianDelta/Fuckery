# Fuckery

Things take their shape from what they are not allowed to do. A snowflake has six arms because of the angle at which water can bond; a road runs where it runs because somebody walked that line first, it packed down, and everybody since has taken the easy way. The second half is the part worth staying for: the shape that forms becomes a restriction in its own right, so whatever forms next forms under conditions the last round left behind, and it never quite comes back to where it started.

That loop is the entire subject of this repository. Its formal name here is **Fuckari**. The framework around it is called **Fuckery**, which is deliberate and explained below.

No philosophy required, and no physics. Just a willingness to look at something ordinary for longer than usual.

## Why it exists

I built it because I needed it for other problems, not because the world was short of ontologies. The [Constraint Protocol](https://github.com/daxfoundation/constraint-protocol) is one of those problems, and this is the thinking that gave it its shape.

It is published rough. There are dead ends in here — some of them me working something out in public and not getting there — and I would rather ship those alongside the good bits than polish the whole thing until it sounds finished when it isn't. I am not an ontologist and don't claim to be. Take what is useful, leave the rest.

## Where to start

[`FUCKERY.md`](FUCKERY.md) is 63,500 bytes. Nobody is expected to read that, and most people shouldn't begin there. Three ways in, shortest first. Each is complete on its own terms, and stopping after any one of them is a normal thing to do.

**Four minutes — the idea, in one object.** [The Snowflake: Fuckari in Action](https://jeyanandan.com/blog/snowflake-fuckari-in-action/). Read as far as *"Let's watch the loop run"* and you have it, drawn on a thing you've seen a few million of. Keep going and it becomes the framework run end to end against an object whose physics nobody disputes — twenty-eight steps, one move each, closing with the four things a snowflake cannot do. Best single thing to send someone.

**Twenty minutes — the spine.** In [`FUCKERY.md`](FUCKERY.md): §1, §2, §3, then stop. §1 is the argument compressed until it barely breathes; it looks dense on first pass, so come back to it after §2. §2 is the seven non-negotiable commitments. §3 is the three primitives nothing else derives from. That's the argument. The rest is what happens when you push on it.

**The whole thing.** [`FUCKERY.md`](FUCKERY.md) — v0.51, 2026-09-10, §0 through §18, 540 lines. It reads better as a workbench than a book: §13 is the dead ends and rejections, §14 is ten open problems held open on purpose with the overclaim each one forbids attached, §16 is the change log showing what every version broke in the one before it.

## The name

It's called Fuckery so that nobody genuflects at it, including me. A framework that sounds authoritative stops getting questioned, and this one needs to keep getting questioned.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Fuckery** | The framework — this repository and the thinking in it. |
| **Fuckari** | The loop it describes: *emergent, top-down-causal, resonance-constraint*, compressed into one word. The mechanism. |
| **Fuckarian** | A being constituted by its assumptions — constrained by them in the same act that lets it think at all. |

## What it is not

Not peer-reviewed. No external adversarial review. Not a doctrine, and it won't tell you what anything means.

It also forbids things on purpose, which is the part I'd rather be judged on. Anything that fits everything explains nothing, so a fair amount of the work goes into what the framework is *not allowed* to say — including the flattering things it would love to say about itself. It does not explain why there is something it is like to be a system running this loop, and it equally forbids the opposite claim, that the question is an illusion. The ten open problems in §14 are the honest part.

Every claim is marked, in spirit if not in tag, as **invariant**, **derived**, **speculative**, **illustrative** or **reasoning trail**. Reasoning trails are first-class here: the path by which a claim was reached is not scaffolding to throw away once the claim stands.
